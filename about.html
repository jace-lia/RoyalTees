ABOUT US

RoyalTees is more than just a clothing brand — it's a way to express who you are. Founded by Syntax Crew, RoyalTees was created with a passion for bringing comfort, style, and individuality together through everyday streetwear.

We believe that fashion is a powerful form of self-expression. That's why we offer versatile, comfortable, and stylish pieces designed for individuals who aren't afraid to embrace their identity and tell their own stories.
